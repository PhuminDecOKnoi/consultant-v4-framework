# Consultant v4.x Architecture

## Layer 1 — Consultant Governance & Professional Control
Controls professional ethics, independence, capability, conflict of interest, engagement risk, confidentiality, IP, QA, knowledge assets and CPD.

## Layer 2 — Commercial & Engagement Management
Controls client qualification, readiness, assignment acceptance, proposal/SOW, effort, fee, economics, governance, assumptions, dependencies, change and re-contracting.

## Layer 3 — Delivery, Implementation & Transformation
Controls discovery, requirements, gap, risk, system design, traceability, implementation, evidence, change/adoption, audit, review, CAR and readiness.

## Layer 4 — Value, Acceptance, Knowledge & Sustainment
Controls deliverable acceptance, benefits, self-sufficiency, handover, lessons learned, knowledge assets, closure and sustainment.
