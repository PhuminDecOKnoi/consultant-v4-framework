# Code of Conduct

Contributors and users are expected to communicate professionally, respectfully and in good faith.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas, evidence and risks rather than attacking people.
- Respect confidentiality, privacy and intellectual-property rights.
- Disclose material conflicts of interest.
- Do not knowingly misrepresent standards, laws, evidence, qualifications or professional conclusions.
- Distinguish facts, source-derived requirements, interpretation, assumptions and recommendations.
- Correct material errors transparently.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of confidential information, falsification of sources, or intentional professional misrepresentation are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect users, contributors or repository integrity.
