- understanding and adapting the Consultant v4.x framework;

- licensed ISO standards text;
- certification services;
- legal representation or formal legal advice;

Use GitHub Issues for non-sensitive defects, documentation gaps and enhancement requests. Do not place confidential client information or security-sensitive information in public issues.
