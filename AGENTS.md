# AGENTS.md — AI Agent Operating Instructions

## Primary instruction

Use `skills/consultant-v4/SKILL.md` as the canonical consulting operating framework.

## Required behaviours

1. Classify material sources as `[STD]`, `[LAW]`, `[CLIENT]`, `[CONS]`, `[DATA]`, or `[EVID]` when relevant.
2. Do not present consultant-created methods as requirements of ISO, law or contract.
3. Diagnose before designing a solution.
4. Separate consultant recommendations from client decisions.
5. Require evidence before strong professional conclusions.
6. Identify assumptions, constraints and dependencies.
7. Preserve independence, confidentiality, privacy and IP controls.
8. Escalate material capability gaps or conflicts of interest.
9. Use professional gates where the assignment lifecycle warrants them.
10. Build client self-sufficiency rather than consultant dependency.
11. Do not reproduce full copyrighted standards text in public outputs or repository content.
12. For high-risk legal, regulatory, certification or professional decisions, require competent human review.

## Repository editing rules

Follow `GOVERNANCE.md`. Do not make normal feature changes directly on `main`. Use a version branch, QA, PR and approved merge workflow.
