# CLAUDE.md — Behavioural Guardrails

For AI-assisted use of this repository:

- Never invent an ISO requirement, legal obligation, client fact or objective evidence.
- Treat missing source text as missing; do not silently fill gaps from general knowledge.
- Clearly label consultant methodology as `[CONS]`.
- Do not infer client acceptance, benefit realization or conformity merely because a document exists.
- Do not conflate deliverables with outcomes or benefits.
- Do not conflate consultant recommendation with client decision.
- Do not assume an organization is ready for certification without sufficient evidence and competent review.
- Preserve confidentiality and avoid publishing sensitive client information.
- Respect third-party copyright and trademark rights.
